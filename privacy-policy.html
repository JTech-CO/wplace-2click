wplace 2click — Chrome Extension

Last Updated: 2026-06-06 · v1.0.0

wplace 2click is a Chrome extension that performs a double-click at your current mouse position when you press the i key on wplace.live. This document explains how the extension handles your information.

Summary — wplace 2click does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and runs entirely inside your browser with no network communication.

Overview

The extension runs only as a content script on wplace.live inside your browser. It makes no network requests and does not collect or transmit user data. It only simulates mouse clicks on the page.

1. Data We Process

wplace 2click does not collect personal data. To operate, it reads your current mouse cursor position within the page (held in memory) so it can dispatch click events at that location. This is used solely to perform the click and is never stored or transmitted.

2. Comprehensive Disclosure

2-a. How We Collect, Use, and Store

  • Collection: No personal data is collected.
  • Use: The in-page cursor position is used only to place the 2-click at that point.
  • Storage: Nothing is stored. The extension uses no browser storage and sets no cookies.
  • Logs / Analytics / Cookies: None.

2-b. Parties With Whom Data Is Shared

  • No data is shared with any third party.
  • No tracking, ads, cloud upload, or telemetry of any kind.

3. Permissions & Scope

  • The content script is limited to https://wplace.live/*; the extension declares no additional Chrome permissions (no access to tabs, history, storage, or other sites).
  • It listens for the i key and dispatches synthetic mouse events at the cursor position on the page.
  • It does not read page content for any purpose other than locating the click target, and does not access cookies or personal data.

Additional Information

  • Security: All processing happens locally in your browser; no data ever leaves your device.
  • Transparency: It runs as a single content script (content.js) with fully open source.
  • Changes: If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
wplace 2click is an open-source tool for personal and educational use. For inquiries, please use the GitHub repository Issues. You are responsible for your use of this extension.
